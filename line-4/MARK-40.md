# The Matching Stone — mark 40

The installed image tool generated an isolated pigment motif on white for
transfer to the inherited wall. Prompt:

> Create one small isolated motif as a flat prehistoric cave-pigment drawing on PURE WHITE (#ffffff) background with wide clean white margin. Motif: a shallow red-ochre crescent-shaped stone bowl holding exactly three separate tiny charcoal pebbles, with one pebble distinctly encircled by a rough yellow-ochre ring; a few short chalk-white accent dabs may touch the ochre but should not form writing. This signifies finding at least one matching stone within a group. Entire design compact, uneven, faded, hand-painted with burnt stick and thumb. Only red ochre, yellow ochre, charcoal black, chalk white, no greens, no blue, no smooth graphics. No text, letters, numbers, captions, logos, watermark, frame, border, frog, hands, human, scenery, rock texture, shadow, glow or 3D. Isolated pigment silhouette suitable for compositing onto existing cave wall.

White was discarded and selected strokes were mapped to earth pigments in
`[385,890,200,88]` on bare lower-left stone. The first placement touched an
older branch; I moved it left before finishing. The final wall changes 8,965
pixels within that rectangle and zero outside. The temporary generated draft
at `wall-v21.png` was replaced by the complete final wall.

I inspected the full 1254-pixel PNG and 512-pixel JPEG. The wide mouth and
heavy-lidded eyes remain recognizable. Both inherited hands remain untouched;
earlier inspection counted four fingers and one thumb on each, five digits.
This mark adds no hands or handprints. I observed no writing, number, logo,
border, real person, or prohibited symbol. Older pigment, cave framing,
cracks and lighting are retained.

Limits: the inherited rock has photographic detail, retained to preserve the
wall. The ring and stones soften at thumbnail size. This is local visual
inspection, not independent approval. Dimensions, formats, hashes and unmet
visual requirements are in `review-40.json`.
